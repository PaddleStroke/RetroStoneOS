Homebrew games bundled with RetroStoneOS by permission of their authors
======================================================================

The games below are NOT under an open licence. Their authors gave 8BCraft,
the maker of the RetroStone2, permission to ship them pre-installed with
RetroStoneOS. The permission covers the RetroStoneOS images of every board
(the RetroStone2 and the Raspberry Pi / Orange Pi images), and nothing else:

  - the ROM files are not in the public RetroStoneOS git repository (they
    are kept in a private repository and added to the images at build time);
  - they must not be redistributed on their own, sold separately, or shipped
    with any other product without asking the author again;
  - all rights stay with the authors.
