Distribution in RetroStoneOS
----------------------------
This folder is the upstream theme, unmodified: the files of
es-theme-gbz35-dark-master.zip (GitHub master, commit aefbbb2),
minus the repository's .gitattributes, plus this LICENSE.txt. RetroStoneOS is
free and open source with public builds, and the console is sold without
firmware: the theme ships in the free firmware image, never in anything sold.

The system logos in */system.svg are trademarks of their respective owners
(Nintendo, Sega, Sony, NEC, SNK, Atari, Bandai...), used to identify the
systems. The fonts in _inc/fonts are Roboto / Roboto Condensed (Google,
Apache License 2.0).

Changes: none. RetroStoneOS's theme engine replaces _inc/fonts/
RobotoCondensed-Regular.ttf (a PostScript Type 1 file) with its own copy of
Roboto Condensed at run time, and uses the folder of a related console for
systems this theme has no folder for (pcenginecd -> pcengine, neocd ->
neogeo, wonderswancolor -> wonderswan, pico -> megadrive); the files are
untouched.

RetroStoneOS addition: retrostone/
----------------------------------
The retrostone/ folder is not part of the upstream theme: it is the
RetroStone system of RetroStoneOS (8BCraft's own games, docs/vc-games.md),
written for RetroStoneOS in the style of this theme. system.png is the
RetroStone2 logo without the "2" and background.png its three stones in two
flat colours, both made from frontend/assets/splash/retrostone2-logo.png
(the RetroStone name and logo belong to 8BCraft); theme.xml follows the
other system folders. The upstream files are untouched.
