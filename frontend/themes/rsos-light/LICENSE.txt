RetroStoneOS "rsos-dark" / "rsos-light" EmulationStation themes
=================================================================

These themes combine two parts under two licences.

1. Console art: _art/carbon/*.svg
---------------------------------
The per-system console and controller drawings (and the three collection
folder icons) come from the "carbon" EmulationStation theme:

  Theme "carbon" by Rookervik, based on "simple" by Nils Bonenberger
  https://github.com/RetroPie/es-theme-carbon (commit b09973e, 2020-05-20),
  files <system>/art/controller.svg.
  The same vectors are the "controller & system vector art" that the gbz35
  theme (by rxbrad) credits to Carbon.

Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike (CC BY-NC-SA),
as stated in Carbon's readme.txt, copied unmodified as
_art/carbon/CARBON-README.txt (its summary line says "2.0"; the legal text it
includes is the 4.0 International licence:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).

Changes made for RetroStoneOS (each file also says so in a comment):
  - every filled shape also gets an outline stroke of its own colour, 4 units
    wide (2 for the collection icons), so the thin lines stay visible when the
    art is drawn ~250 px wide;
  - editor metadata (Illustrator/Inkscape) removed;
  - the files are named after Carbon's system folders (snes.svg, sg-1000.svg...);
  - zxspectrum.svg: the "sinclair" wordmark of the original drawing is removed,
    and the hidden reference layer (an embedded JPEG) of scummvm.svg is dropped.
The theme draws them tinted with each system's accent colour.

These adapted files are distributed under the same CC BY-NC-SA licence:
you may share and adapt them if you credit Rookervik (Carbon) and Nils
Bonenberger (simple), indicate changes, do not use them for commercial
purposes, and share adaptations under the same licence. RetroStoneOS is free
and open source with public builds, and the console is sold without
firmware: these themes ship only in the free firmware image.

Console shapes are depictions of third-party products (Nintendo, Sega, Sony,
NEC, SNK, Atari, Bandai, Coleco, MSX, Commodore, Amstrad, Sinclair, IBM
PC...); no logos or trademarks are drawn.
